Questions

Asked by almost everyone

Do I need to go to the RTO myself?

Only for the steps the law needs you there for — your driving test and, in some states, the learner's test or a photo and signature. Everything else — forms, slots, fees, follow-up — we do for you.

What exactly will I pay?

Two things: our service charge, which we tell you in writing before we start, and the government fee, which we pay at actuals and hand you the official receipt for. Nothing else is added later.

I have never driven. Which course should I take?

Most complete beginners do well with Confident Driver (21 sessions). If you're nervous in traffic or want highway and night practice, pick Road Master. You can always add extra sessions.

Can I take the driving test in your car?

Yes. Our dual-control cars carry all the papers the RTO asks for, and your instructor goes with you on test day.

Do you have lady instructors?

Yes — just ask when you book. There is no extra charge.

How long until I get my licence?

Your learner's licence must be at least 30 days old before the driving test. After you pass, the smart card usually arrives by post within a few weeks, and we track it for you.
